# RayRay Arena - Art Direction

## Core Visual Philosophy

Keep RayRay Arena simple, readable, and functional.

Gameplay readability is more important than decorative complexity.

Do not turn prototype assets into elaborate concept art.

---

# Environment

Visual theme:

Frontier planetary / lunar environment.

Use:

- rock
- dust
- lunar terrain
- simple space installations
- clear faction coloration

Rock walls define territory boundaries and funnels.

Walls are terrain.

Walls:

- cannot be attacked
- cannot be destroyed
- cannot be moved

---

# Bases

All bases initially share the same basic design.

Concept:

A small frontier planetary habitat / mining outpost.

Primary structure:

Low hemispherical protective dome.

The dome is designed conceptually to withstand:

- harsh environment
- debris
- pressure
- exposure

It is NOT designed as a military fortress.

Bases should NOT have:

- guns
- turrets
- missiles
- defensive weapons

The warriors defend the base.

Small environmental details are acceptable:

- airlock
- antenna
- communications equipment
- vents
- storage/mining modules

Keep bases visually compact.

The gameplay map identifies each base with only a small BTC, ETH, SOL, or BNB
logo/emblem in nearby terrain. Do not show faction names or letters beside the
logos, or the old "___ BASE" labels. Do not show "CENTRAL COMBAT ARENA" text in
the central diamond. These are artwork-only changes; base and terrain geometry
remain unchanged.

---

# Faction Identity

Prototype factions:

BTC
ETH
SOL
BNB

Faction identity should primarily use color.

BTC:
orange family

ETH:
blue / violet family

SOL:
purple / cyan / green family

BNB:
yellow family

Avoid overwhelming the terrain with saturated faction colors.

Faction identity should remain easily readable during combat.

---

# Units

All factions initially share ONE fundamental unit design.

The unit is a simple humanoid planetary worker / warrior.

The silhouette should include:

- helmet
- compact body
- arms
- legs
- boots
- oxygen / life-support pack
- weapon

Keep the design recognizable at very small gameplay size.

Do not create an elaborate military soldier.

The character should look like a planetary worker capable of defending an outpost.

---

# Unit Progression

The fundamental body stays almost unchanged.

Do NOT create radically different character forms for each equipment tier.

ATK progression:

Primarily represented by weapon changes.

Major visual milestones:

5
10
15
20

At higher milestones:

- weapon becomes visibly more capable
- oxygen/life-support equipment may become subtly larger

DEF progression:

May be represented through subtle helmet/protection changes.

Do not add large amounts of armor.

After 20:

ATK and DEF may continue increasing numerically.

Visual asset progression remains capped at the final equipment tier.

---

# Faction Unit Variations

Faction warriors should remain fundamentally identical.

Faction identity comes from:

- suit accent color
- weapon accent color
- faction marker if necessary

Do not create faction-specific mechanics or radically different warrior bodies during V0.1.

---

# Prototype Art

V0.1 does NOT require finished artwork.

Temporary:

- circles
- simple sprites
- basic silhouettes
- primitive buildings

are acceptable during gameplay testing.

Approved reference artwork should guide later production art.

Do not sacrifice prototype progress for visual polish.

---

# Visual Rule

If a unit cannot be identified clearly while dozens of units are fighting simultaneously, the design is too complicated.

Simple is intentional.

# V1 Unit Design

## Canonical Unit

RayRay Arena V1 uses one simple canonical unit design for all four factions.

The unit is a small planetary spaceman / worker capable of defending the faction's base.

The unit should look distinctive and appealing without being elaborate.

## Basic Appearance

The V1 unit should have:

- Simple humanoid proportions
- Slightly compact / sturdy body shape
- Classic bubble-style space helmet
- Dark visor
- Simple pressure suit
- Gloves
- Boots
- Small oxygen / life-support pack
- Pickaxe used as the unit's basic tool / weapon

The unit should NOT have:

- Heavy military armor
- Guns
- Large shoulder armor
- Elaborate electronics
- Capes
- Wings
- Excessive accessories
- Highly detailed equipment that becomes unreadable at gameplay size

The unit should look primarily like a planetary worker rather than a professional soldier.

The pickaxe reflects the frontier / mining-outpost setting.

## Gameplay Readability

The unit must remain easily recognizable when displayed at small gameplay size and when many units occupy the battlefield simultaneously.

The important visual silhouette is:

Bubble Helmet + Spacesuit + Oxygen Pack + Pickaxe

The design should prioritize a strong silhouette over small decorative details.

## Faction Variations

All factions use the exact same basic unit design and proportions.

Faction identity is communicated through suit/accent color.

Initial factions:

- BTC - orange accent
- ETH - blue / violet accent
- SOL - purple / cyan / green accent
- BNB - yellow accent

Faction coloring must remain clearly distinguishable during large battles.

Faction color does NOT affect gameplay statistics.

## V1 Upgrade Artwork

V1 does NOT require different unit artwork for ATK or DEF upgrades.

The same unit sprite may be used throughout the entire match.

ATK and DEF progression are gameplay/statistical systems first.

The following remain future visual-polish options:

- stronger-looking weapons at ATK milestones
- subtle helmet changes at DEF milestones
- slightly larger oxygen equipment at equipment milestones

These visual changes are NOT required for V1.

Do not delay V1 development to create them.

## V1 Combat Tool

The canonical V1 unit carries a pickaxe.

For V1:

Pickaxe = tool + weapon.

This reinforces the idea that these units are frontier planetary workers defending their bases rather than purpose-built soldiers.

## Design Rule

Keep the V1 unit simple.

Do not redesign the unit during implementation without explicit approval.

Gameplay comes first.
