# Purpose

This document defines the canonical visual identity of RayRay.

Keep it simple.

RayRay has been designed and approved.

Future artwork may place RayRay in different environments, lifecycle stages, poses, lighting, and stories, but should preserve his recognizable identity.

---

# Canonical Reference

The approved RayRay hero image is the primary visual reference for the character.

This image defines:

- RayRay's lunar face;
- token body;
- proportions;
- arms and hands;
- legs and shoes;
- metallic construction;
- Solana-inspired color treatment;
- friendly visual style;
- astronaut helmet;
- overall level of realism versus cartoon styling.

Do not redesign RayRay without an intentional decision to change canon.

# Canonical Token

RayRay is the token.

RayRay is not a mascot representing a separate token.

The token itself is the character.

The token consists of a metallic outer ring surrounding a complete lunar face.

The Moon surface forms RayRay's face naturally.

The face should resemble a friendly interpretation of the traditional "man in the moon" concept rather than a cartoon face pasted onto a coin.

The lunar surface, craters, coloration, eyes, facial features, and overall appearance shown in the canonical reference should remain consistent.

---

# The Full Moon Rule

RayRay's lunar face is always physically complete.

The Moon is never cut into a crescent.

The Moon is never replaced by a crescent.

The lunar surface is never physically removed to represent Moon phases.

Crescent and partial-Moon appearances may only be created through:

- lighting;
- shadow;
- orientation; or
- environmental conditions.

The complete RayRay Moon remains underneath the shadow.

# Core Art Direction

RayRay should feel:

- friendly;
- approachable;
- slightly whimsical;
- polished;
- dimensional;
- adventurous;
- optimistic;
- capable of carrying humor without becoming a joke.

RayRay may exist in cartoon-like situations, but RayRay himself should retain the polished dimensional style established by the canonical artwork.

The humor should primarily come from RayRay's circumstances and behavior.

---

# Solana Visual Language

RayRay uses a Solana-inspired color language:

- aqua;
- cyan;
- green;
- violet;
- purple;
- light metallic tones; and
- iridescent transitions between these colors.

The colors should complement the metallic token construction and lunar surface rather than overwhelm them.

---

# Astronaut Identity

Graduation makes RayRay an Astronaut.

Astronaut RayRay retains the same token body and lunar face.

The astronaut helmet shown with the canonical character is part of the post-Graduation visual identity.

RayRay may carry, wear, hold, or place the helmet nearby depending on the scene.

RayRay does not need to be inside a spacecraft to be portrayed as an Astronaut.

# Lifecycle Artwork

The website and social content may show earlier stages of RayRay's life.

Possible lifecycle scenes include:

1. Born
2. Growing
3. Dreaming
4. Backyard Bootstrap Engineering
5. Graduation
6. Astronaut RayRay

Earlier lifecycle artwork may make RayRay appear younger through context, environment, scale, posture, equipment, and story.

Avoid turning RayRay into an excessively childish character.

RayRay should always remain recognizable as the same character.

# Backyard Bootstrap Engineering

This is an important part of RayRay's early visual story.

RayRay dreams about space before having the resources to properly reach it.

Scenes may include:

- a backyard workshop;
- tools;
- sketches;
- telescope equipment;
- salvaged parts;
- homemade rocket components;
- prototypes;
- repairs;
- repeated attempts; and
- RayRay looking toward the sky.

RayRay's engineering should be questionable but not ridiculous.

The correct visual reaction should be:

> *"...you know what? That might actually work."*

---

# Core Character Principle

RayRay is:

> **A dream + some tools + questionable engineering + determination.**

RayRay is not incompetent.

RayRay builds.

RayRay learns.

RayRay repairs.

RayRay tries again.

Whether RayRay ever reaches THE MOON is unknown.

Trying is the adventure.

# Canonical Hero Scene

The approved hero composition includes:

- RayRay as the primary foreground character;
- RayRay's astronaut helmet nearby;
- Planet Solana environment;
- a rocket launching in the background;
- THE MOON visible in the sky;
- the RayRay flag;
- the slogan:

> **A SMALL TOKEN WITH A BIG DREAM**

- and milestone/destination signs representing possible future adventures.

The background rocket does not need to belong to RayRay.

The scene may simply depict RayRay watching someone else launch and imagining what might someday be possible.

# Text Rule

RayRay does not ask:

> WEN MOON?

Other people ask RayRay:

> **WEN MOON, RAY?**

RayRay dreams about THE MOON.

When appropriate, RayRay's response may simply be:

> *Someday...*

---

# Visual Freeze Rule

The canonical RayRay design is frozen.

New artwork should change:

- scene;
- pose;
- activity;
- lifecycle context;
- lighting;
- clothing/equipment when appropriate; or
- environment.

New artwork should not casually change:

- RayRay's face;
- Moon surface;
- token construction;
- fundamental proportions;
- identity;
- character style; or
- canonical engraving.

**Create new RayRay stories.

Do not create a new RayRay.**
