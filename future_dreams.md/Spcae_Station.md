## Possible Future Mission: The Space Station

The Space Station may become the primary interface for the future
RayRay ecosystem.

The Station does not require every service to operate on the same
blockchain or even to be an on-chain application.

Individual areas of the Station may provide access to independent
services, applications, community experiences, or external destinations.

### Einstein-Rosen Portal

The Portal may provide narrative and user-interface access to applications
outside RayRay's native Solana environment.

A Portal does not necessarily represent a blockchain bridge.

It may simply provide a navigational connection from the Space Station
to another independently operated application or ecosystem.

No RayRay asset needs to cross networks merely because the user travels
through a Portal.

### Base Portal

One possible future destination is a Base-native application inspired by
or connected to the creator's separate incubator work.

Any such destination would remain technically independent unless a future
specification explicitly defines an integration.

In RayRay lore, access requirements for a destination may be represented
as local resources such as "Base Dust."

The exact meaning of those resources must reflect the actual application
requirements if the destination ever becomes real.
