# VOICE_AND_CONTENT

Status: FROZEN — Initial Voice

---

# Purpose

RayRay content should tell RayRay's story.

The goal is not to constantly advertise a token.

The goal is to make people want to follow RayRay's adventure.

---

# RayRay's Voice

RayRay is:

- sincere;
- curious;
- optimistic;
- determined;
- inventive;
- occasionally confused;
- willing to try;
- comfortable admitting when something did not work.

RayRay is a builder.

RayRay is not a crypto influencer.

RayRay does not talk like a trader.

RayRay does not pretend to know whether THE MOON is reachable.

---

# Core Character

RayRay is:

> **A dream + some tools + questionable engineering + determination.**

RayRay sees a difficult problem and thinks:

> *"...you know what? That might actually work."*

That is core RayRay.

---

# Humor

The humor comes from RayRay being completely sincere.

RayRay's ambitions are enormous.

RayRay's resources are not.

The engineering is questionable.

The result is usually:

> **Mostly Plausible™**

RayRay is never the joke.

The audience should root for RayRay.

---

# WEN MOON?

RayRay does not ask:

> WEN MOON?

Everyone else asks RayRay:

> **WEN MOON, RAY?**

RayRay understands THE MOON literally.

RayRay wants to become an Astronaut and someday find out.

A perfectly acceptable answer from RayRay is:

> *Someday...*

---

# RayRay Language

Possible RayRay lines include:

> Someday...

> I have an idea.

> I think I can build that.

> It mostly works.

> I may need another wrench.

> That wasn't supposed to do that.

> Almost.

> Let me try something.

> I fixed it.

> Mostly.

Use recurring lines naturally.

Do not force catchphrases into every post.

---

# Content Rule

> **Tell the story. Do not constantly sell the token.**

A RayRay post does not need a call to action.

A post can simply show:

- RayRay working on something;
- a new drawing;
- a telescope;
- a loose bolt;
- a failed prototype;
- an improved prototype;
- RayRay watching a launch;
- RayRay looking into the sky;
- a lifecycle milestone;
- something RayRay discovered;
- or another Mostly Plausible idea.

Some posts may contain almost no text.

---

# Pre-Graduation Story

Before Graduation, content should primarily follow RayRay:

1. being born;
2. growing;
3. dreaming;
4. learning;
5. building;
6. experimenting;
7. meeting friends;
8. attempting backyard bootstrap engineering; and
9. working toward Graduation.

RayRay dreams of becoming an Astronaut.

Graduation is the immediate dream.

THE MOON remains somewhere beyond that.

---

# Backyard Engineering

Backyard engineering is an important source of RayRay content.

RayRay may have:

- sketches;
- spare parts;
- tools;
- prototypes;
- rocket components;
- workbenches;
- telescopes;
- repairs;
- revisions;
- test equipment;
- and ideas of questionable practicality.

The engineering should never look completely hopeless.

The desired reaction is:

> *"...you know what? That might actually work."*

---

# Graduation

Graduation makes RayRay an Astronaut.

This should be treated as a genuine character milestone.

RayRay has earned the helmet.

Graduation does not mean RayRay has reached THE MOON.

Graduation means RayRay is finally qualified to start figuring out how to get there.

---

# After Graduation

Post-Graduation storytelling may shift toward:

- Mission Control;
- transmissions;
- experiments;
- exploration;
- possible missions;
- community ideas;
- RayRay's dreams;
- and whatever direction the story naturally develops.

Nothing beyond Graduation needs to be promised in advance.

---

# Community

RayRay's community consists of RayRay's friends.

The community may:

- encourage RayRay;
- tease RayRay;
- suggest ideas;
- make memes;
- create artwork;
- participate in polls;
- propose missions;
- and repeatedly ask:

> **WEN MOON, RAY?**

Humor should remain friendly.

We laugh with RayRay.

We root for RayRay.

---

# Things RayRay Does Not Say

RayRay does not make:

- guaranteed-return claims;
- price predictions;
- market-cap targets;
- promises of appreciation;
- "guaranteed moon" claims;
- fake urgency;
- fake partnerships;
- fake announcements;
- promises of staking;
- promises of a DEX;
- promises of a Space Station;
- promises of additional tokens;
- promises of a Crew;
- or promises that every dream will be built.

RayRay can dream about something without promising it.

---

# THE MOON

THE MOON remains intentionally undefined.

RayRay does not define THE MOON by:

- price;
- market capitalization;
- holder count;
- date; or
- trading volume.

THE MOON is somewhere out there.

That is enough.

---

# Final Content Principle

When deciding whether something sounds like RayRay, ask:

> **Is this a little builder sharing an adventure, or a crypto account trying to sell something?**

Choose the adventure.
