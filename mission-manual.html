Purpose

RayRay's Mission Manual is the technical reference for the RayRay project.

The RayRay website tells the story.

RayRay's Mission Statement explains the philosophy.

This Mission Manual documents how the actual project works, what exists today, what remains undecided, and how RayRay's Dreams may someday develop into real Missions.

The governing rule is simple:

RayRay's story never outruns RayRay's reality.

RayRay may dream about many things.

The Manual may grow with the adventure, but reality always leads the story.

3. Token Authorities

Solana token implementations may include authority roles such as Mint Authority and Freeze Authority.

Mint Authority controls whether additional token units may be created.

Freeze Authority may allow the authorized party to freeze compatible token accounts.

Authority roles may be changed or permanently revoked depending on the final token implementation.

RayRay's preferred approach is to remove unnecessary authority after the final token configuration has been established.

The final authority configuration will be disclosed in this Manual when it can be verified on-chain.

Until then:

Mint Authority: TBD

23. Security and Durable Rules

RayRay should be built so the most important token rules do not depend on ongoing trust.

Where the selected launch infrastructure permits, the final configuration should seek to establish durable, publicly verifiable rules.

The design objective includes:

  • fixed token supply;
  • permanently revoked Mint Authority where applicable;
  • permanently revoked Freeze Authority where applicable;
  • publicly disclosed creator allocation;
  • publicly disclosed creator fees;
  • no undisclosed transfer restrictions;
  • no unnecessary administrative token extensions;
  • verifiable post-Graduation liquidity treatment; and
  • clear disclosure of any permissions that remain.

On-chain facts should be described as final only after they can be verified.

RayRay's foundational token rules should remain understandable and durable as the project grows.

The creator may continue to:

  • maintain RayRay's website;
  • create artwork and stories;
  • communicate with the community;
  • receive disclosed creator fees;
  • propose future Missions; and
  • develop separate optional applications.

Those creative and operational responsibilities are separate from the foundational rules of the token itself.

The goal is not to remove RayRay's ability to grow.

The goal is to separate growth and creativity from unnecessary control over the token's foundational rules.

Build the important rules to last. Then keep building the adventure around them.
