# Purpose

Ray Ray will launch as a simple meme and community token.

The launch exists to introduce Ray Ray, establish his community, and allow his story to begin.

The launch must preserve the character, world, and principles established in:

- `RAY_RAY_CANON.md`

Implementation should remain intentionally minimal.

---

# Launch Rule

> **If Ray Ray does not need it to be born, it is not a launch requirement.**

Ray Ray does not need an ecosystem on Day 1.

He needs a character, a story, a home, and some friends.

Everything else can wait.

---

# Launch Principles

The preferred launch should provide:

- a fair and transparent launch mechanism;
- no hidden creator token allocation;
- clearly disclosed platform and trading fees;
- transparent creator-fee mechanics;
- transparent bonding-curve mechanics, if applicable;
- clear Graduation requirements;
- permanently locked or burned Graduation liquidity where possible;
- publicly verifiable token and liquidity activity;
- freedom for Ray Ray to remain an independent project after Graduation; and
- minimal technical and financial overhead.

Creator revenue generated through transparently disclosed platform or trading fees is acceptable.

Ray Ray should not depend on selling a creator allocation to fund continued development.

# Initial Launch Scope

Ray Ray launches as:

- one token;
- one character;
- one story;
- one website;
- one community; and
- one dream.

That is enough.

---

# Explicitly Out of Launch Scope

The initial launch does not require:

- custom smart contracts;
- staking;
- yield programs;
- a custom swap;
- a custom DEX;
- a Space Station;
- a bridge;
- additional tokens;
- The Crew;
- cross-chain functionality;
- governance;
- a DAO;
- NFTs or collectibles;
- complicated tokenomics; or
- a promised post-Graduation ecosystem.

These ideas may exist in Ray's dreams without becoming implementation requirements.

---

# Growing Up

Before Graduation, Ray Ray is growing up on Planet Solana.

His primary objectives are simple:

1. Be born.
2. Make friends.
3. Grow with his community.
4. Dream about space.
5. Graduate.
6. Become an Astronaut.

Community activity may include:

- memes;
- artwork;
- Ray Ray stories;
- social posts;
- community conversations;
- polls;
- simple events; and
- other lightweight activities.

Community activity should not unnecessarily consume development time needed for other work.

---

# Graduation

Graduation represents Ray Ray becoming an Astronaut.

Graduation is an achievement in itself.

The Graduation cap comes off.

The Astronaut helmet goes on.

> **Ray Ray has officially become Astronaut Ray.**

Graduation does not require the immediate development of additional utilities or infrastructure.

Graduation completes the initial Ray Ray roadmap.

---

# Ray's Dreams

Ray begins dreaming about space before Graduation.

His dreams may include:

- 🚀 Blast Off
- 🌕 THE MOON
- 🛰️ The Space Station
- 👨‍🚀 The Crew
- 🌌 Exploration
- 🪐 Strange new worlds
- ❓ Things Ray does not understand yet

These are narrative possibilities.

They are not launch commitments.

They may inspire future community discussions or development if Ray Ray develops sufficient community, activity, creative momentum, or genuine reasons to continue building.

---

# After Graduation

No fixed post-Graduation implementation roadmap is required at launch.

After Graduation, the project may evaluate:

- community activity;
- holder interest;
- trading activity;
- creator resources;
- available development time;
- community ideas;
- creator ideas; and
- whether any proposed utility solves a real need.

Only then should additional development be authorized.

---

# Mission Control

If Ray's adventure continues after Graduation, the community may become:

> **MISSION CONTROL**

Mission Control may receive:

- transmissions from Ray;
- mission reports;
- discoveries;
- polls;
- questions;
- memes;
- community missions; and
- requests for input.

Mission Control is a community concept.

It does not automatically represent formal token governance or a DAO.

---

# Blast Off

Graduation makes Ray an Astronaut.

It does not automatically send him into space.

Blast Off requires a reason to continue.

Possible reasons may include:

- an active community;
- continued interest;
- creative momentum;
- sustainable project activity;
- a useful ecosystem idea; or
- something genuinely worth building.

If none of those exist, nothing needs to be manufactured simply to satisfy an old roadmap.

---

# THE MOON

THE MOON has no official:

- token price;
- market capitalization;
- date;
- deadline; or
- financial definition.

THE MOON is part of Ray Ray's story.

The community may decide for itself what reaching THE MOON means.

Until then:

> **WEN MOON, RAY?**

---

# Future Development Rule

Future functionality must justify its own existence.

A feature should not be built merely because it appeared in one of Ray's dreams.

For example:

- A Space Station should not be built unless the project benefits from one.
- A Crew should not be created unless additional characters, utilities, or tokens have genuine purposes.
- Cross-chain functionality should not exist unless Ray Ray has a genuine reason to interact with another ecosystem.
- Staking should not exist merely because meme tokens are expected to have staking.
- A DEX should not be built simply because a roadmap once mentioned one.

Ray's dreams create possibilities.

They do not create technical debt.

---

# Success

Ray Ray does not require a large ecosystem to be considered complete.

The initial Ray Ray story succeeds if:

- Ray launches;
- people understand and enjoy the character;
- a genuine community has the opportunity to form; and
- Ray has the opportunity to Graduate and become an Astronaut.

Anything beyond that is another adventure.

---

# Scope Protection

Ray Ray exists as a deliberately lightweight project.

Complexity should only be introduced when justified by actual project needs.

Before adding a feature, ask:

> **Does Ray Ray actually need this now?**

If the answer is no:

Don't build it.
